    How to play
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Three litters got mixed up. Use the children's traits and the clues to work out which two children belong to each couple.
    
      
    Tap once to rule out a couple ✕, twice to choose it ✓, and again to clear the mark.

    Genes & alleles
    
      
    Each trait in Genigma is controlled by a gene. For each trait, an individual has two alleles: one inherited from the mother and one from the father.
    
      
    Genotype vs phenotype
    
      
    Genotype is the pair of alleles an individual has, such as BB, BW or WW. Phenotype is the visible trait produced by that genotype, such as black, gray or white coat.
    
      
    Homozygous
    BB
    same alleles    WW
    same alleles    Homo
    = same
    
      
    Heterozygous
    BW
    different alleles    PR
    different alleles    Hetero
    = different
    
    
    
    
    
      
    How inheritance works
    
      
    Each parent passes one of their two alleles to each child. So if one parent is BW and the other is WW, their children can be BW or WW, but not BB. Use impossible combinations to rule couples out.
    
      
    Incomplete dominance
    
      
    In Genigma, heterozygous genotypes have their own intermediate phenotype. This means all three genotypes can look different.
